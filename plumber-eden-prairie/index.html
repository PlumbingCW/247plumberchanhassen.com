Softened city water changes the advice you get here

.

Fifteen city wells feed the system, and the treatment plant removes hardness before the water reaches you, leaving a tap average near 5 grains (90 mg/L). Raw well water measures closer to 21 grains (360 mg/L). Utility staff tell residents a home unit is generally not needed. In practice, a plumber should not push a softener on you before you test your own tap.

If you add a softener, it ties into both supply and drain, and the plumbing code has a say. The UPC lineage behind Minnesota's code usually calls for an air gap on the softener's drain line, which stops dirty water from being pulled back. Your plumber confirms what applies, and Eden Prairie building staff answer inspection questions.

A Chanhassen neighbor gets different advice because that city's water is not softened. Tankless units there need regular descaling, while in Eden Prairie the interval is often longer. If you want the full picture, read about tankless water heaters or ask us whether a softener or filter fits your situation.

Bluffs, Purgatory Creek and Rice Marsh Lake: where the water goes

.

Eden Prairie sits on the north bank of the Minnesota River valley, and bluffs, Purgatory Creek and Round Lake and Staring Lake parks shape how water moves across the land.
