Call the 24/7 line

.

The fastest way to reach a plumber is to call. The line is open around the clock for emergencies, and the dispatcher can book a same-day appointment when available for problems that are not urgent. There is no contact form on this site, because a form cannot tell you to close the main valve.

Use the call button at the top of the page or in any call-to-action band. It dials the 24/7 line straight from a phone.

Have these ready when you call

.
  • The address where the problem is, including unit number or the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening in plain words: leaking, backed up, no hot water, noisy, smelly.
  • Whether the main water valve is shut off and whether that stopped the flow.
  • Which fixtures or rooms are affected.
  • A callback number in case the call drops.
  • Photos on your phone if you can take them safely. Do not wade into standing water near outlets.

None of this has to be perfect. If you do not know where the main valve is, say so and the dispatcher will talk you through looking for it.

Email for non-urgent questions

.

For questions that can wait, write to contact@247plumberchanhassen.com. Good topics include scheduling a planned water heater swap, asking whether a town is covered or sending photos after a visit. Please do not email about active leaks, sewage or gas smells, because email is not monitored all night.

Keep card numbers, passwords and similar private details out of any email you send. A dispatcher will tell you how to handle payment when the work is done.

A few things that help a visit go faster

.

Clear a path to the problem if you can. Move stored boxes away from the water heater, the cleanout or the sink cabinet, and secure pets in another room. Plumbers work faster, and more safely, with room to move.

If the problem is outdoors, such as a suspected water service leak near the street, do not dig. Minnesota law expects a call to Gopher State One Call at 811 before digging so buried lines can be marked.

Where we are based

.

The dispatch base sits at 761 W 78th St, Chanhassen, MN 55317. This is a dispatch base, not a walk-in showroom, so please call before visiting. Plumbers travel to your home or business rather than working from a counter.
