Dispatched from Chanhassen

.

The dispatch base is 761 W 78th St in Chanhassen, MN 55317, in the heart of town. Plumbers head out from here to Chanhassen addresses and to the towns around it. The line works 24/7, so the service area does not change after dark.

Chanhassen itself covers about 24 square miles. Most of the city is in Carver County, but 154 acres on the eastern edge sit in Hennepin County. If your address is in that corner, the county line affects permitting and utilities, not whether we can reach you.

Excelsior and Shorewood share ZIP 55331 because the post office uses the Excelsior city name for both. Do not be surprised if a bill or a package shows one town when you live in the other. Give the dispatcher your street address and the town you actually live in.

Every town on this list works under the same state plumbing code, which starts from the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), yet each city runs its own building inspections. The plumber explains which rules fit the work, and your own city office confirms local steps.

The five town pages

.

Water source, housing stock and quirks all vary by town, so each gets its own page. You will find the details for Chaska, where the city lists hard water at 26 grains per gallon, and Excelsior, with its Victorian-era homes along Water Street.

Shorewood, covered on its own plumber page, includes homes on city water and homes on private wells. In the west, Victoria runs its own water, sewer and stormwater. East of Chanhassen, the Eden Prairie plumber page explains why the city softens its water and what that means for your heater.

How dispatch works when you call

.

You call

The dispatcher asks for your address, town and the problem in plain words.

Address check

Dispatch confirms the address is inside the area and notes the nearest cross street.

Safe-first advice

While the plumber is assigned, you get advice on valves, fixtures and photographs.

A plumber is assigned

A licensed plumber leaves Chanhassen for your address. Drive time depends on traffic, weather and the hour.

Price before work

At your address the plumber finds the cause and states the cost up front.

Dispatch does not promise an arrival time because roads and weather change. It can tell you honestly whether the problem needs a truck right now or can wait for a same-day appointment when available.

In that case, ask for a referral to a licensed plumber closer to you, or look one up with the state's Check a License tool.

Outside the area

.

If you live beyond these towns, ask the 24/7 line. For a planned job such as a water heater swap, the answer is often yes with a little notice. For a late-night emergency, the nearest licensed plumber is usually the better call, because a long drive adds delay while water keeps running.

Before any work, it is reasonable to ask which licensed plumber will come and to check that license. The state's Department of Labor and Industry keeps a public lookup for exactly this purpose.
